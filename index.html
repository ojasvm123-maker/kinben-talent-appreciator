About KinBen Talent Appreciator

KinBen Talent Appreciator is an AI-powered platform designed to identify talented athletes from villages and small towns across India. Using smartphone video recordings and intelligent performance analysis, the platform creates standardized scores that allow sports academies to discover hidden talent remotely.
